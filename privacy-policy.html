Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how Mimz Ride collects, uses, stores, discloses and protects personal information when you visit our website, make a chauffeur booking, arrange payment or contact us. Our handling of personal information is subject to applicable Nigerian data protection law.

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mimz Ride website and the chauffeur booking services offered through it. For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the person or organisation that determines why and how personal information is processed is referred to in this policy as the data controller.

The data controller responsible for Mimz Ride is:

  • Legal name of operator: Mimz Inc.
  • Business contact address: No. 4B Town Hall Mall, Barnawa, Kaduna
  • Privacy contact: mimzride@gmail.com

These details must be completed before this policy is published as the final privacy notice. References to "Mimz Ride", "we", "us" or "our" mean the operator identified above.

2. Personal Information We Collect

We collect information that is relevant and reasonably necessary to operate our website, manage chauffeur bookings, process payments, communicate with customers and meet applicable legal obligations. Depending on your interaction with us, this may include:

  • Identity and contact information: Your name, telephone number and email address.
  • Booking information: Your selected service, booking date, pickup time, requested duration, pickup location, any drop-off location you provide, booking reference and notes submitted with your booking.
  • Payment and transaction information: Payment amount, currency, transaction reference, payment status, transaction identifier and relevant payment timestamps.
  • Communications: Information contained in enquiries, complaints, cancellation requests, refund enquiries and other messages you send to us.
  • Technical and security information: Where generated or made available by our website, hosting provider or security tools, this may include IP address, browser and device information, access logs, error reports and information used to investigate suspicious activity.

We do not ask you to submit payment card PINs, online banking passwords or other confidential authentication credentials through our booking form. Please do not include such information, or unnecessary sensitive personal information, in booking notes.

Where information is necessary to assess or fulfil a booking, failure to provide it may prevent us from confirming or delivering the requested service. We will not treat every requested field as compulsory if it is not necessary for the relevant purpose.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may process personal information for the following purposes:

  • To receive, assess, manage and confirm booking requests.
  • To calculate the price of the selected chauffeur service.
  • To initiate payments, verify payment outcomes and reconcile transactions with the relevant bookings.
  • To contact you about confirmations, pickup arrangements, delays, changes, cancellations or other matters affecting your booking.
  • To respond to questions, complaints, refund requests and service enquiries.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of our website and to investigate suspected fraud, misuse or unauthorised access.
  • To maintain business, financial and transaction records and comply with applicable legal, tax, accounting or regulatory obligations.
  • To establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary.

We will not use personal information for a new purpose that is incompatible with the original purpose unless a lawful basis under applicable law permits the further processing. Where the law requires additional information or consent, we will provide it or obtain that consent as applicable.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under section 25 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, personal data may be processed where a recognised lawful basis applies. Depending on the circumstances and the specific processing activity, we may rely on:

  • Contract or pre-contractual steps: To take steps you request before a booking agreement is formed, or to perform an agreement relating to your booking.
  • Legal obligation: To comply with a legal obligation that applies to the operator, including applicable financial, tax, accounting or regulatory requirements.
  • Legitimate interests: Where necessary for legitimate business interests, such as securing the website, preventing misuse, resolving disputes or protecting legal rights, provided those interests are not overridden by your fundamental rights and freedoms.
  • Consent: Where you have given valid consent for a specific processing purpose and consent is the appropriate lawful basis.
  • Vital interests or other legally recognised grounds: Where the particular circumstances satisfy the conditions provided by applicable law.

We will determine the appropriate lawful basis for each relevant processing activity rather than treating all processing as consent based. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it, subject to applicable law. Withdrawal does not retrospectively make processing carried out lawfully before withdrawal unlawful.

5. Payments and Paystack

Where online payment is available, the payment process may involve Paystack or another payment provider expressly used for the transaction. You may be redirected to a payment provider's hosted checkout to complete payment.

The payment provider may collect and process information needed to authenticate, process and verify your transaction, subject to its own applicable terms, privacy notice and legal obligations.

Mimz Ride's server may retain the booking and transaction information needed to associate a payment with a booking, verify the payment outcome, reconcile the amount due and investigate discrepancies. We do not intentionally store full payment card numbers, card PINs or online banking passwords in our booking database.

We cannot make representations about all information held by a third-party payment provider. You should review the provider's applicable privacy information before submitting payment information through its service.

6. When We Share Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information that is necessary and proportionate to the relevant purpose to the following categories of recipients:

  • Payment providers: To initiate, process, authenticate and verify transactions.
  • Drivers and service personnel: To the extent necessary to arrange and deliver your chauffeur booking, including providing relevant pickup and contact details.
  • Technology and infrastructure providers: Hosting, database, security, website maintenance and other providers that support the operation of the service.
  • Communication platforms: Where you choose to contact us or communicate with us through WhatsApp or another third-party service.
  • Professional advisers and competent authorities: Where necessary for legal advice, dispute resolution, compliance with a lawful request or protection of legal rights.

Where a third party processes personal information on our behalf, we will take the measures required by applicable law to govern that processing, including appropriate contractual and security safeguards where required.

Information will not be disclosed merely because it is available to us. Any disclosure must have an appropriate purpose and lawful basis.

7. WhatsApp and External Websites

Our website may provide links to WhatsApp, payment services or other external websites. These services may be operated independently of Mimz Ride and may have their own privacy notices, terms and security practices.

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, the information you submit may be processed by WhatsApp and its relevant service providers in accordance with their applicable policies. Information shared through a third-party platform may also be subject to that platform's technical and operational arrangements.

We recommend sharing only the information reasonably necessary to handle your enquiry. We are not responsible for the independent processing practices of external services, although this does not exclude any responsibility imposed on us by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements and the circumstances of the processing.

Booking and transaction records may need to be retained for service delivery, customer support, payment reconciliation, accounting, taxation, legal compliance, dispute resolution and the establishment or defence of legal claims.

The applicable retention period will depend on the type of record, the purpose for which it is held, relevant legal requirements and whether a dispute or other lawful reason requires continued preservation. We will take reasonable steps to delete or securely anonymise information when it is no longer required, unless its retention remains lawful and necessary.

9. Security and Personal Data Breaches

We will implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, having regard to the nature of the information and the risks involved, to protect personal information against unauthorised or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, damage, alteration or disclosure.

Depending on the systems actually in use, safeguards may include restricted access, secure server-side payment verification, security headers, request-rate controls, access monitoring and appropriate protection of stored records.

No website, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will assess and respond to it in accordance with the notification, reporting and other obligations that apply under Nigerian data protection law.

If you reasonably believe that your personal information has been exposed, accessed or misused in connection with our service, please contact us using the privacy contact details below.

10. International Processing and Transfers

Some payment, hosting, communication or technology providers may process personal information outside Nigeria, depending on the services used and their infrastructure.

Where personal information is transferred from Nigeria to another country, we will assess and comply with the applicable requirements of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, including sections 41 to 43 on transfers of personal data outside Nigeria. This may require an appropriate level of protection, an applicable transfer mechanism or another lawful condition for the transfer.

Where required, we will provide information about the relevant recipients, countries or transfer safeguards and take appropriate measures to protect the information. The safeguards applicable to a particular transfer will depend on the recipient, destination, processing arrangement and applicable law.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to the conditions, exceptions and limitations under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Receive information about how your personal information is collected and processed.
  • Request confirmation of whether we process your personal information and request access to that information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Request erasure of personal information where the legal conditions for erasure are satisfied.
  • Request restriction of processing in circumstances provided by law.
  • Object to processing where a right to object applies, including certain processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Request data portability where the statutory conditions are met.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Benefit from applicable protections relating to decisions based solely on automated processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

These rights are not absolute in every circumstance. For example, we may lawfully retain certain records to meet legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims, even where deletion has been requested.

To make a request, contact us using the privacy contact details in section 15. Please describe your request clearly and provide enough information for us to locate the relevant records. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity or authority to act on behalf of another person.

We will handle requests within the time limits and in the manner required by applicable law. Where we cannot comply with a request, we will explain the position to the extent required by law.

You may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission to obtain information about your rights or submit a complaint.

12. Children's Personal Information

Our chauffeur booking service is intended for people who can lawfully make or authorise the relevant booking. We do not knowingly seek unnecessary personal information about children.

Where a booking involves a child or personal information relating to a child, we will process that information only where a lawful basis and any additional safeguards or authorisation required by applicable Nigerian law are satisfied.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in circumstances that are not appropriate, please contact us so that we can assess the matter and take any action required by law.

Our website may use technical mechanisms necessary to provide requested functionality, support security, maintain a booking flow or remember temporary booking information.

The technologies actually used may change as the website develops. If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.
