Questions, answered

How is the price calculated?

Your total is calculated by multiplying the hourly rate by the number of hours booked, subject to a two-hour minimum. The rate depends on whether you use your own vehicle or the chauffeur’s.

Can I extend my booking on the day?

Extensions are subject to availability. Contact us on WhatsApp to check whether additional hours can be arranged.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Available payment methods are displayed during checkout and are processed through the configured payment provider.
