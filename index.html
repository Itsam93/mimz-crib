Questions, answered

How is the price calculated?

Your total is calculated by multiplying the hourly rate by the number of hours booked, subject to the two-hour minimum. The rate depends on whether you use your own vehicle or the chauffeur-and-vehicle service.

Can I extend my booking on the day?

Extensions are subject to availability. Contact Mimz Ride on WhatsApp to check whether additional hours can be arranged.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Available payment methods are displayed during checkout and processed through the configured payment provider.

Where does Mimz Ride operate?

Mimz Ride serves Kaduna and beyond, with driving experience across more than 12 Nigerian states, including the FCT. Share your pickup and destination details when booking to discuss your journey.
